Side Story 6.2: Morning in Mindis Hall

Kel walked into the elegantly decorated room. Just as expected, he found the owner of the room behind a study desk covered in numerous documents and books.

The owner was a man who was dressed in humble clothing, but ones that suited his status. He sat quietly, gently, and serenely behind the study desk, and as he flipped through the pages, creating rustling sounds, he silently read the book in his hand.

Kel arrived before the wide desk in three large strides. He found an empty space on the desk, then jumped onto the table to sit on it in an extremely inelegant fashion.

He winked and grinned at the owner of the room, who sighed and put down his pen to cast him a glance before he shook his head in resignation. Then, he lowered his head again to read the book in his hand once more.

Kel raised his eyebrow in response. He did not have to be wary in this place, and neither did he have to worry.

Because the person sitting here… was his kindest and most amiable eldest brother.

Kel turned his head around and was astonished to find a bottle of grape wine and a few goblets beside the study desk, which had official documents piled up like mountains on it. By the looks of it, the wine was a popular brand from Sera Dukedom.

Kel unceremoniously grabbed the bottle of wine and stated, “I remember that you didn’t like drinking wine.”

There was no need for small talk between them, no need for greetings, and no need for those meaningless formalities…

Because a long time ago, this place had been his sanctuary, his safe haven, and the last place he truly considered as his home in Eternal Star City.

It was especially so after his mother passed away.

“But Horace and Herman like it, so do you.” His eldest brother did not raise his head. His voice was as pleasant to the ears as ever. While it was not as pleasant as Herman’s, whose voice sounded like a melody, there was a unique and gentle quality to it. It made others subconsciously able to calm down their restlessness and be at peace.

Kel picked up the wine bottle, filled a goblet, and shrugged. “Then… thanks for the hospitality.”

His eldest brother hummed, flipped a page, and grabbed  a scroll. Kel sipped some wine, and at the same time he mocked his brother’s taste in wine.

He reacted to the other two names his eldest brother just mentioned.

“So, what special occasion is it today? A day for prisoners to go outdoors?” Kel walked to the window and cast a glance outside. He did not find the two despicable figures who were in cahoots with each other. They might have left.

“Musclehead, Sissy, and that damned Fatty… How is it that once the stubborn old fool leaves, all the strange creatures in the capital flock to you?”

His eldest brother chuckled, and the chuckles vibrated in the air. They were gentle and infectious. “Don’t scold yourself that way.”

In one second, Kel understand the mocking tone in his brother’s words, and once he understood it, he scratched his head. “Alright.”

If Herman and Horace had mocked him this way, Kel would have definitely mocked them back rudely. But this came from his eldest brother, and it was just a small joke.

Their words were different, and the person who said them was also different.

“Why did they come to you?”

His brother scribbled something on paper and took out another piece of paper. “I sought them. You must understand, father went forth for an inspection. Now, all government affairs must be directly reported to me.”

Kel understood what he meant. “Oh, no wonder Horace had such a sour face. Certainly s*cks for him.”

He might have said this, but in his heart, he was very happy.

Kel then realized that he had just cursed. He quickly covered his mouth and silently said a word to his eldest brother in apology.

Sorry.

As usual, his eldest brother did not get angry. His gaze remained on the paper. He only curled his lips and smiled faintly.

This was exactly how he had reacted when Kel intentionally tore his eldest brother’s book when he was still little; gentle, calm, tolerant, and brilliant.

Kel believed that no matter how great a disaster he created, or how problematic an issue he caused, even if he killed the King of Eckstedt, his eldest brother would probably react like this. He would shake his head gently, smile faintly, and tell him “I’m here, don’t be afraid. Just go rest.”

And he would always have a way. Always.

When he thought of this, Kel noticed that he had drained his wine. He filled up his goblet again, and during that time, he glanced at the document in his brother’s hand. It looked like a letter from a foreign noble.

He knew that these things were major national matters, but his brother did not seem to be concerned about it.

“What’s that?”

His brother rubbed at the crease between his eyebrows. “Returning Wind Castle and Lush Forest are protesting against the land tax.”

Kel blinked. “Is it really bad?”

“This concerns the conflict of administrations between Blade Edge Hill’s traditional, established customs, and the bill Seucader passed during the era of the Virtuous King. I plan to send Bancroft there for mediation purposes next season. On the surface, this is an imbalance of the tax rates between the new and old nobles, but in truth…”

His brother suddenly raised his head and revealed his gentle face. His calm expression gave him a cordial air, and it seemed like it would never disappear.

“Are you interested in it?”

Kel raised his hands as a sign of surrender, and shook his head like a rattling drum. “I don’t even understand what you’re saying… unless… it has something to do with me?”

Sometimes, he felt that he was very lucky. He never had to deal with these troublesome governmental affairs. He always had an eldest brother who could do everything to shield him from all these problems.

And even more fortunately for him, his eldest brother was the crown prince, the heir to the throne, and the future master of Constellation.

‘If anyone else inherited the throne, such as a lunatic like Horace or a useless fool like Herman… Oh, Sunset Goddess, I’d rather launch a rebellion and tarnish my reputation. I don’t ever want to see that happen.’ Kel swore in his heart. ‘Hmm… maybe I can tolerate Uncle John on the throne.’

“Well, I do think that there is.” His eldest brother creased his brow and thought in apparent seriousness. A worried look appeared on his face.

“Five years into the future, the ladies from Returning Wind Castle will be even more eager to get married.”

Kel’s expression changed drastically in an instant. “My goodness, that problem is indeed very serious.”

His eldest brother watched his reaction and could not help but laugh as he shook his head. During that second, his eldest brother’s lips curled into a beautiful arc. There was laughter in his eyes, as if all the optimism in the world was contained in them.

‘Herman may be called a “beauty” and drove people mad for him, but that’s definitely because Midier doesn’t find the need to compete with him,’ Kel thought.

Because when his eldest brother smiled, he was more good-looking, gentler, more brilliant… and more charming than anyone else.

Kel smiled as well, and he did so naturally. If he smiled like this outside, he would have definitely scoffed at himself for smiling so foolishly. He would only smile like this inside this room.

His eldest brother put down the work in his hand and picked up the cup of flower tea by his side.

“I remember that you are still under house arrest, and are supposed to reflect on your actions.”

Kel instantly tensed up.

He hated being under house arrest, and he hated the stubborn old fool even more. He hated the person who placed him under house arrest, that was why when anyone asked him about his house arrest while the punishment was still in effect, even if Kel was displeased, he would show a rebellious look, as if he was not bothered by it.

But his eldest brother… before his eldest brother…

Kel, who was usually eloquent and could pester someone endlessly, stuttered—something which rarely happened to him.

“Urk… yeah. In all honesty, I came here to talk about this matter. Um, it might just be a short night, but I think, um, I think that I have benefited greatly from my reflection. I have gained great results from it…”

As expected, his eldest brother laced his fingers together and said seriously, “So, are you ready to tell me the truth behind why father placed you under house arrest?”

“And by that, I mean… besides discussing literature and poetry with beautiful ladies and madams, while naked.”

‘Great!’ he screamed madly in his head. ‘Who else in all of Errol doesn’t know this secret? I’d really like to know who else doesn’t know. Who else?! Come out! Come on out… I’ll tell you!’

While Kel was immersed in a world he dubbed “Kel’s tiny world made from his ego”, a sudden commotion rose from outside the window. It attracted the two people’s attention.

A sharp voice traveled into the air, it seemed to have been from a maid.

“Princess! Princess! Please, wait!”

Kel shuddered!

Embarrassed, he immediately seized the opportunity to change the topic. He rushed to the window and looked outside the window in an exaggerated fashion.

“No way, you brought Connie out from the palace?”

His eldest brother shrugged. Clearly, he saw through his plan, but did not say anything else.

Kel stuck his head out the window. A girl dressed in strange clothes that made her look like she was stuck between two cardboard pieces ran on top of the roof of Mindis Hall’s wing.

She nimbly slipped through the arms that tried to stop her and completely disregarded the pale, distraught expressions of the attendants and guards.

The girl was around eleven or twelve years old. She had an exquisite face, and her hair danced in the air. She did not have any shoes on, and her feet were covered in dirt. Her entire being radiated happiness and madness, a stark contrast to the despair and horror on the other people’s faces.

“Don’t worry. This is the newest version of my creation, after I modified it according to the ancient books. I call it, ‘Connie’s Wings V6’, there won’t be any problems to it. Just watch as I run into the wind and fly—”

While the girl nimbly moved through their arms, she shouted in a cocky manner. Her joyful tone was shrill and her pitch high. All of Mindis Hall, from the manor to the courtyard, could hear her clearly.

She also attracted more guards to her side. Quite a number of them were panicking and at a loss, and they were Royal Guards who did not understand the girl.

The girl ran madly as she flapped the two cardboard pieces. She neared the edge of the roof…

“Princess, don’t!”

As the maids screamed at the top of their lungs and the guards cried out in surprise and disbelief, the girl jumped off the roof in the direction of the sun!

“AAAHHHH!” she screamed excitedly, flapping her arms. Her long hair danced, and the hems of her dress fluttered. “I’m going to fly—”

In the next second, the girl drew a beautiful arc in the air, and then…

…she plunged down into a thicket.

The rustling sound of branches moving arose, and Kel, who was watching all of this with his mouth hanging open, heard that frightening, dull sound.

*Thud!*

Kel shuddered violently. He shrank back, and his features contorted together.

‘That looks… painful.’

The maids’ hysterical cries rang through all of Mindis Hall.

“AAAAHHHH! SOMEONE!”

“THE PRINCESS! THE PRINCESS FELL AGAIN!”

Thus, a large group of people pushed and shoved at each other as they fought down the stairs to be the first to reach the thicket. Their footsteps were loud. They sounded like a marching band.

Once he finished watching all this, Kel, still yet to recover from his shock, finally managed to shut his mouth before he forced himself to smile in an unpleasant manner.

“Are you… really not going to do something about this?”

At some point of time, his eldest brother had resumed working on governmental affairs. He worked at a moderate pace, and flipped through a warrant indifferently. He hummed in acknowledgement.

“Do something about what?”

Kel pointed at the spot under the window and grimaced. He saw Morion and Rogo rush to the scene. “Um… about how we should stop our troublemaking little sister from destroying Mindis Hall?”

His eldest brother snorted and cast him a glance, the one he had whenever Kel pulled a prank on him when he was little. “Don’t be afraid. Aida will take good care of her.”

‘Don’t be afraid.’ When he heard these words, Kel was dazed for a moment, and he found himself longing for the past. ‘But… what did Midier just say? Aida?’

At that moment…

“AARRGGHHH!!! MOVE!”

As if to verify his eldest brother’s words, another lithe and petite figure bounded from the window while making a big fuss over nothing. She wailed in grief, and she was so loud that she could shake the skies and the earth.

“No no no no no! Young Connie, young Connie, you have to be well!”

*Thud!*

“Young Connie, my good Connie, wake up, wake up! Alright, as long as you wake up, I won’t call you bad Connie anymore!”

That terrifying voice he and his brothers heard since they were little (and apparently, the stubborn old fool, the stubborn old fool’s father, and the stubborn old fool’s grandfather also heard when THEY were young) shot through the heavens carelessly, just like it always had. But this time, it was done in a wretched, tragic manner.

“Don’t diiiiieee! I only went to steal a drumstick from the scullery, and I came back right afterwards, how could you…? Hic… I’m so sooooorrrryyyy… If you’re still alive, wake up, wake up!!!”

Kel leaned against the window and watched the hooded figure shake something vigorously. The corners of his lips started twitching, though he was unaware of it.

The Royal Guards and maids around her wanted to approach her in their panic, but they were thrown off by the hooded figure when she flung her arm at them.

“Aaaahh!!”

A few seconds later, a soft, gentle voice that could only belong to a girl cried out in surprise. It then grew louder with each passing second. “Cough, cough, Aida, I think… you’re pushing down on my breASTS… AAAHH!”

She sounded really pitiful, as if she had been wronged.

The Royal Guards around them looked as if they had been scalded by boiling water. They turned around and resumed their positions as if nothing had happened.

Kel sighed.

As expected, the owner of the terrifying voice who had just been crying carelessly cried again, and this time, she sounded especially thrilled.

“Hic… what?… AAAHHH, THIS IS GREAT! Young Connie, you’re still alive! Hic! Rubbish, hic, how could you have something as evil as breasts…”

That soft, female voice sounded weak.

“I’m telling you the truth. The books say I won’t grow up like this…”

‘…Right. Aida also followed her. Then she must be taking good care of Constance… my foot!’

Kel howled in his heart. He had his suspicions that half of his younger sister’s “abnormal activeness” was born out of that hooded shortie’s influence!

Kel left the window and walked back, feeling speechless. He picked up his goblet, but that terrifying voice belonging to a certain Aida rose again.

“AAAAHHHH YOUNG CONNIE, I WON’T EVER LEAVE YOU AGAIN! Oh, by the way, do you eat drumstick? It’s still piping hot. Come on, open your mouth! Say, let me tell you a secret I just heard, but you have to promise not to tell…”

“Um… Aida… my breasts…”

“Say, do you know why your youngest elder brother is under house arrest? Because he crawled under the blankets with thirty women in their birthday suits…”

*Pfft!*

Kel spat his wine on the carpet before he started coughing violently.

‘F*ck me! I shouldn’t have come today!’

With swift movements, Kel drew the curtains in the room, blocking off Aida’s thunderous voice outside. He stared at his eldest brother. Both were speechless. Kel could only weep in his heart.

After a long period of silence, his eldest brother finally sighed and broke the silence.

“You understand Connie.”

It was as if he had not seen how flustered Kel was. Kel’s eldest brother sighed and said, “She is very lonely, and she longs for other people’s attention and acknowledgment.”

Kel had been immersed in “Kel’s tiny world made from his ego” as he coughed nonstop, but he still heard the words, and he was stunned. He raised his head and met his eldest brother’s gaze.

At that moment, his eldest brother’s gaze was no longer the same as usual. It was not just gentle, tolerant, and calming; there was something else in his eyes.

His eldest brother said faintly, “That’s why she urgently wants to do something, whatever it is. We have to understand that feeling… We should understand it.”

Kel fell silent. He originally opened his mouth with the intention to say something, but his eldest brother simply stared at him like he had seen through everything.

So, Kel lowered his head.

After some time, the commotion outside the window finally died down. Kel evened his breathing and let out a long sigh.

“It’s all thanks to that stubborn old fool.” He snorted softly before he wrapped his arms around his chest. He deliberately let resentment seep into his voice. “Over the past ten something years, he acts like he has no daughter.”

This was unusual.

Usually, Kel would not argue with him, because his eldest brother would be in the right most of the time. Even if he was wrong, there was still a reason behind his error.

But on this day, when his eldest brother used that strange gaze to stare at Kel, he suddenly felt like there was something stuck in his throat, and if he did not get it out, he would be very uncomfortable.

“Father…?”

Midier turned around and had his back face his brother. Kel mocked his eldest brother in return, “Just what sort of father would hate his own child?”
